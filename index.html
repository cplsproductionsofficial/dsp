IMPORTANT

This website is currently a front-end prototype. It does not actually deliver music to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music or other DSPs, issue ISRCs, collect royalties, or create legal distribution agreements. Those functions require a legitimate distribution partner/backend.
